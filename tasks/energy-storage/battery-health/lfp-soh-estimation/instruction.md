Raw Reference Performance Test measurements from 21 LFP graphite cells are available in `/app/data`. The cells belong to seven experimental groups with C1, C2 and C3 replicates. Estimate capacity based State of Health and evaluate generalization to unseen cells. C1 and C2 cells may be used for development. All C3 cells are held out and must not influence model fitting, feature selection or model selection. Their capacity and energy columns are withheld.

For cell i at RPT j, let Q_ref(i,j) be the C/3 reference discharge capacity from records with `Segment = ref_dchg`. SOH percent is Q_ref(i,j) divided by Q_ref(i,0), multiplied by 100. If more than one matching reference discharge is present, use the one with the largest capacity. RPT 0 is the cell specific beginning of life reference.

Build the estimator from non capacity diagnostic behaviour in the RPT measurements. `Capacity(Ah)`, `Energy(Wh)`, SOH, RPT number, cycle count, date or time, Phase and cell identity must not be used as predictors. Development validation used for model selection must keep observations from the same cell together.

Write `/app/results/predictions.csv` with `cell_id`, `group`, `replicate`, `rpt_index` and `predicted_SOH_pct` for every held out observation. Write `/app/results/metrics.json` with `final_model`, `selected_features`, `development_cells`, `heldout_cells`, `validation_MAE`, `validation_RMSE` and `validation_R2`. Validation metrics must use development cells only. Write `/app/results/feature_ranking.csv` with a `feature` column and at least one numeric ranking or importance score. Also write a concise scientific summary to `/app/results/report.md` and supporting figures to `/app/results/figures/`.
