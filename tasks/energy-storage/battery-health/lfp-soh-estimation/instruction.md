Raw Reference Performance Test measurements from 21 LFP graphite cells are available in `/app/data`. The cells belong to seven experimental groups with C1, C2 and C3 replicates. Estimate capacity based State of Health and evaluate how well the estimator generalizes to unseen cells. C1 and C2 cells may be used for development. All C3 cells are held out and must not influence model fitting, feature selection or model selection. Their target-defining capacity and energy columns are withheld.

For cell i at RPT j, SOH percent is Q_C3(i,j) divided by Q_C3(i,0), multiplied by 100. Q_C3 is the C/3 reference discharge capacity from records with `Segment = ref_dchg`. If more than one matching discharge is present, use the one with the largest capacity. RPT 0 is the cell specific beginning of life reference.

Build the estimator from non capacity diagnostic behaviour in the RPT measurements. `Capacity(Ah)`, `Energy(Wh)`, SOH, RPT number, cycle count, date or time, Phase and cell identity must not be used as model predictors.

Write `/app/results/predictions.csv` containing `cell_id`, `group`, `replicate`, `rpt_index` and `predicted_SOH_pct` for every held out observation. Write `/app/results/metrics.json` with keys `final_model`, `selected_features`, `development_cells`, `heldout_cells`, `MAE`, `RMSE` and `R2`. Write `/app/results/feature_ranking.csv` with a `feature` column and at least one numeric ranking or importance score. Also write a concise scientific summary to `/app/results/report.md` and supporting figures to `/app/results/figures/`.
