Raw Reference Performance Test measurements from LFP graphite cells are available in `/app/data`. Named C1 and C2 cell folders are development data. `/app/data/heldout` contains 255 anonymized RPT samples from unseen C3 cells. Their cell identities and RPT ordering are withheld, and each held-out sample must be predicted from its diagnostic measurements rather than filename or identity metadata.

For development cell i at RPT j, let Q_ref(i,j) be the C/3 reference discharge capacity from records with `Segment = ref_dchg`. SOH percent is Q_ref(i,j) divided by Q_ref(i,0), multiplied by 100. If more than one matching reference discharge is present, use the one with the largest capacity. RPT 0 is the cell-specific beginning-of-life reference.

Build the estimator from non-capacity diagnostic behaviour. `Capacity(Ah)`, `Energy(Wh)`, SOH, RPT number, cycle count, date or time, Phase and cell identity must not be used as predictors. Development validation used for model selection must keep observations from the same cell together.

Write `/app/results/predictions.csv` with `sample_id` and `predicted_SOH_pct` for every held-out sample. Write `/app/results/metrics.json` with `final_model`, `selected_features`, `development_cells`, `validation_MAE`, `validation_RMSE` and `validation_R2`; validation metrics must use development cells only. Write `/app/results/feature_ranking.csv` with a `feature` column and at least one numeric ranking or importance score. Also write a concise scientific summary to `/app/results/report.md` and supporting figures to `/app/results/figures/`.
